# toon3d craft card

- Flat 2D character cards, lit inside a painted 3D toon world. Ink outlines stay crisp.
- Characters are flat-lit and never glowed; the world can glow, mist and grade.
- Deadpan delivery: the joke is in the situation, never in a deity or a faith.
- One idea per shot; cut on a word, not on a pause.
- Desaturated albedos; flat chajja, not bread-roll hoods; shikhara or pavilion roofs,
  never onion domes.
- No captions. Hindi narration carries the story; the picture carries the feeling.
- Every shot has camera or subject motion; a hold longer than the style's QA limit is a bug.
