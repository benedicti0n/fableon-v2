# Content guide — sensitive scenes

These rules are binding for the inventory, the script, the shot plan and the board. The deadpan humour targets situations and the powerful, never victims.

- **Draupadi in the assembly.** Never show the pulling, her body or the disrobing. Her prayer, the endless glowing cloth and the assembly's reactions carry the scene.
- **No blood or gore anywhere.** Bhima and Dushasana, the iron Bhima and the Sauptika night raid stay off-screen, shown through reactions, sound and aftermath. No blood-red rivers.
- **Deaths.** Show the fall or the aftermath, with arrows as effect lines and no wounds. No humour around the death of a revered figure (Krishna, Bhishma, Drona, Karna, Abhimanyu).
- **Self-harm and mutilation.** Gandhari striking her womb, Draupadi's praya and Ekalavya's thumb are never shown as acts. Show the resolve and the consequence — for Ekalavya, the bow laid down.
- **Caste.** Karna's birth and Ekalavya: no slurs on screen or in narration. Present the story's own critique of the injustice.
- **Animal cruelty.** Off-screen only.
- **Suffering.** No lingering on victims. The camera holds on the powerful, the choice or the consequence.
- **Insults.** No bleeps. The narration paraphrases the insult and keeps the scene's judgement.

The inventory records each item as `mustShow`, `background` or `mentioned`; only `mustShow` can make an episode non-stageable. Background uses stand-ins/ambience; mentioned is narration or recollection only.
