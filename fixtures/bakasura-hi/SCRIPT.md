# "Bhima and Bakasura" — a Fableon short
Format: ~48 s vertical short, Hindi narration, our own wording.
Topic: Bhima and Bakasura: the demon's dinner who ate the dinner first

## Facts (verified against the recorded sources)
1. A demon named Vaka (Bakasura) ruled the town and country near Ekachakra, living on human flesh. (S1)
2. The tribute fixed for the demon was a cart-load of rice, two buffaloes, and a human being to carry it to him. (S1)
3. Householders took turns sending the tribute, and a family's turn came only after many long years. (S1)
4. Anyone who tried to dodge the tribute was killed by the demon along with their family. (S1)
5. The Pandavas were living disguised in the house of a Brahmana at Ekachakra when the tribute turn fell on their host. (S1)
6. Bhima alone ate half of all the food the Pandavas collected while begging. (S1)
7. Kunti offered one of her five sons to go to the demon in the Brahmana's place. (S1)
8. Kunti told the Brahmana that Bhima was immensely strong and would not be killed by the demon. (S1)
9. Bhima carried the demon's food to the forest and began eating it himself while calling the demon by name. (S1)
10. The demon arrived to find Bhima eating the food intended for him. (S1)
11. The demon demanded to know who was eating his food in front of him. (S1)
12. Bhima smiled in derision and kept eating without looking up, even after the demon struck him on the back. (S1)
13. Bhima finished the whole meal, washed, and then stood up cheerfully to fight. (S1)
14. The fight began with the two hurling trees at each other until the area was stripped of trees. (S1)
15. Bhima finally broke the demon's back across his knee, and the demon died. (S1)
16. Bhima made the dead demon's relatives promise to stop killing human beings. (S1)
17. Bhima dragged the dead demon to a town gate and left, unseen by anyone. (S1)
18. In the morning the townspeople found the demon dead and were astonished at the feat. (S1)
19. The Brahmana hid the truth, crediting a nameless mantra-skilled Brahmana with carrying the food and killing the demon. (S1)
20. The townspeople established a festival in remembrance of their deliverance. (S1)
21. The demon is described as huge, red-eyed, red-bearded, with a mouth stretching ear to ear. (S1)

## Script (voice, ~178 words)
एक राक्षस ने अपना भोजन मंगवाया, लेकिन उस भोजन ने ही राक्षस का खाना खा लिया।
एकचक्र गाँव का नियम सीधा था। बकासुर राक्षस ढेर सारा खाना और एक इंसान मांगता था।
गाँव वालों के लिए अच्छी बात यह थी कि मौत की बारी सालों में एक बार आती थी।
बुरी खबर थी कि इस बार ब्राह्मण मेज़बान की बारी थी। कुंती बोली, "मैं अपने बेटे को भेजूंगी।"
कुंती ने भीम को भेजा, जिसे इसमें दावत दिखी। वह जंगल जाकर राक्षस का सारा खाना खा गया।
बकासुर वहाँ पहुँचा तो भीम उसका खाना खा रहा था। उसने पूछा कि यह बेवकूफ इंसान आखिर है कौन?
दरअसल, भीम बहुत ताकतवर था। उसने आराम से खाना खत्म किया और फिर बकासुर की कमर तोड़ दी।
यह ऐसा था जैसे आप पिज़्ज़ा मंगवाएं, और डिलीवरी वाला ही पिज़्ज़ा खाकर आपको पीट कर चला जाए।
भीम ने राक्षस की लाश को घसीटकर नगर-द्वार पर छोड़ दिया। सुबह गाँव वाले हैरान रह गए।
गाँव वालों ने खुशी से त्योहार मनाया। बकासुर की जानलेवा दावत हमेशा के लिए खत्म हो गई।
